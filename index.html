The Ouroboros theorem

encrypt(ROT13(P)) = encrypt(P)

Each step adds two consecutive letter values. ROT13 adds 13 to each; together they vanish modulo 26.

Valid odd-letter rings have two readings. Valid even-letter rings have 26. The decoder shows all of them; it cannot know which one you meant.

ASCII letters turn in the ring. Other Unicode stays exactly where it was. This is an artistic classical cipher, not modern encryption.
